ParkAware – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2025

ParkAware (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info (name, email, sign-in)
  • Subscription/purchase info
  • Device info and crash reports
  • Location data (when permission granted)
  • Optional user content

2. In-App Purchases, Free Trials & Subscriptions

Handled securely through the Apple's App Store, or Google's Play Store; manage in your account settings.

3. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly in User Settings. Some anonymized data may be retained for legitimate purposes.

Alternatively, you may request account and data deletion by emailing us at kilovativedesigns@gmail.com from the same email address linked to your ParkAware account. Please use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request". Once verified, we will delete your account and associated data from our systems.

4. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide and improve the App, manage accounts and subscriptions, send necessary notifications, and ensure security.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with service providers, for legal reasons, or in business transfers.

6. Your Choices

You can update your information, revoke location access, delete your account, and manage subscriptions at any time.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate safeguards to protect your information, but please note that no method is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion, contact us at: kilovativedesigns@gmail.com
